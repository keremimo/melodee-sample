# Shortcut tables

## Buttons

| Button | Press | With **SHIFT** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SAMPLE** | Stage, the track's sound pages; on Stage, its first page. Held: Pads or Keys, and **B1**–**B3** open Stage's sections; + pad: select a kit pad without playing it. + **MUTE**: light show on or off; + **−** / **+**: its style. | Input Config |
| **SEQ** | Sequences, Patterns, Roll or Motion, the last open on the track; on one of them, Sequences. Held: the pads launch the selected track's patterns (**SHIFT** + pad: a sequence on every track), the encoder sets the tempo, **B1**–**B3** open Sequences, Roll and Motion. | Step Edit; again, back |
| **PAD FX** | Pad FX. With **KNOB FX**: the mixer. | Flex Beat |
| **KNOB FX** | Knob FX mode on or off. With **PAD FX**: the mixer. | Effect chains, from the track's (on the mixer, the selected channel's); again, the next along |
| **PAD BANK** | Tap: next bank (on Sequences and Patterns, and with **SEQ** held: the bank of patterns; on Pad FX: the other bank of punches; on Chop: the next 16 slices; on Roll of a keygroup, plug-in or MIDI track: the pads an octave up). A hold never changes the bank. On the mixer: the tracks, or the submixes, buses, input and master. Held + pad: select a track; **K1**–**K3**: its level, pan and send A. Held + **B1**: the mixer on the tracks. Held + **B2** / **B3**: move or copy a track. Held + **ERASE** + pad: clear a track. | Previous bank (on Roll: the octave down) |
| **ERASE** | Alone: erase the selection (on Song, the clip; in the sample browser and the plug-in picker, delete the file or folder from the card). Held + pad, knob, step or fader: erase it, or set it back. Held + an empty kit pad: close the gap. Held + **SEQ RECORD** on a kit: erase the selected pad's notes. Held + **CHOP** on Chop: take every cut out. | Copy |
| **NOTE REPEAT** | Tap to latch, hold for momentary. On Roll: ratchet the marked notes. On Song: repeat the clip to the next marker. | On Roll and Step Edit: write the arpeggio as notes |
| **CHOP** | Chop the last pad; on Chop, back to Trim. On Roll and Step Edit: split the notes. On Song: split the clip. | Note On / One Shot. On Roll and Step Edit: legato. |
| **MUTE** | Mute mode. Tap to latch, hold for momentary. On Roll: mute the marked notes. On the mixer's FX: the lit effect off or on. | Unmute all |
| **LOOP** | Loop the last pad. On Roll and Step Edit: loop the bars in view. On Song: loop the clip's bars. Held: the pads control the Looper. With **16 LEVELS**: open the Looper. | Reverse (on Roll and Step Edit, the notes; on Patterns, the track's direction FWD, REV, RND). Hold 0.5 s: Looper on or off. |
| **16 LEVELS** | 16 Levels on or off. Held: **K1**–**K3** its settings, **−** / **+** its octave, **B1** **SCALE**, **B2** **CHORDS**; on Chords **B2** **FILE** and **B3** **SET**. | 16 Levels type |
| **SAMPLE SELECT** | Sample browser on a kit or keygroup track, plug-in picker on a plug-in track, sounds on a MIDI track. On Step Edit with a step held: the sounds on the pads for that step. Again: closes it. | Save Sample. On the browser: Save Bank. On a MIDI track: Save sound. |
| **TAP TEMPO** | Tap tempo. Held + encoder: the tempo. Held alone: Tempo page; held again there: closes it. | Metronome Off, On, Rec |
| Encoder push | Open, or next card (acts when let go). Held: the Menu. | Back |
| **−** / **+** | Octave, bank, or list. On the mixer: held, the card (**+**) or the strips (**−**). | Undo / redo. Hold **SHIFT** + **−**: the undo list. |
| **SAMPLE RECORD** | On a kit: record onto a pad. On a keygroup, plug-in or MIDI track: the PRINT sheet. On an audio track: its **INPUT**. With **SEQ** held: freeze patterns. | Audio Recall |
| **SEQ RECORD** | Arm or stop recording | Recall |
| **STOP** | Stop. Again while stopped: all notes off. | All notes off |
| **PLAY** | Play from the start (on Song, from the cursor). Held while playing: Fill. | Continue |

Roll's marked notes are in [The pattern editors](sequencing.md#the-pattern-editors). The light
show is in [Light show](basics.md#light-show).

## SHIFT + pads

| Pad | Job | Pad | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Full Level on or off | 9 | Fader |
| 2 | Half Seq | 10 | Rec Quantize on or off |
| 3 | Double Seq | 11 | Resample (on a plug-in or MIDI track: AUTO SAMPLE) |
| 4 | Count-In on or off | 12 | Song |
| 5 | Compressor | 13 | Trim Sample |
| 6 | Half Speed | 14 | Time Correct |
| 7 | Double Speed | 15 | Warp: Time stretch or Pitch |
| 8 | Settings (MIDI Config is one page) | 16 | Project |

On the sample browser, **SHIFT** + pad loads the selected file onto the next
empty pad instead.
